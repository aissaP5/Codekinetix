01 — The approach

Dentistry, the way it
should feel.

We believe a smile is the most personal thing you wear. Our practice pairs the discipline of clinical dentistry with the patience of an atelier — slow, intentional, and quietly excellent.

01

Slow is faster

We schedule 90-minute first visits. We don't rush diagnosis, and we never start treatment on day one unless you ask us to.

02

Planned in software

Digital smile design, intra-oral scanning, and printed models mean you see the outcome before any instrument touches you.

03

Finished by hand

Ceramics layered by our lab technician, restorations sculpted freehand. The plan is digital; the craft is human.

02 — Treatments

Six disciplines,
one studio.

Every treatment is led by Dr. Bekkar, supported by our in-house hygienist and lab technician. No silos. No handoffs to people you've never met.

01Two visits · 14 days

Ceramic Veneers

Hand-layered feldspathic ceramic bonded to your natural enamel — colour-matched, light-correct, and shaped to your face.

026 — 9 months

Invisible Aligners

A digitally planned sequence of clear aligners that move teeth in 0.25 mm steps — discreet, removable, monitored weekly.

033 — 5 months

Implant Dentistry

Single-tooth titanium implants with custom zirconia crowns. Guided placement, immediate temporisation, soft-tissue sculpting.

0490 minutes

Whitening Atelier

In-studio LED-activated whitening paired with a take-home tray system calibrated to your enamel's natural ceiling.

05Single visit

Restorative Care

Direct composite bonding, inlays and onlays crafted by hand, replacing what time has worn without removing what is healthy.

0645 minutes

Hygiene & Prevention

Air-polishing, ultrasonic debridement and a 30-minute radiographic review — the quiet backbone of everything else we do.

03 — The studio

In residence

Dr. Inès Bekkar

A dentist who
draws before she
drills.

Principal Dentist · Founder · DDS, Paris VII · MSc Aesthetic Dentistry, King's College London

Inès founded Lumière in 2013 with a simple conviction: that dentistry should feel closer to a tailor's studio than to a hospital corridor.

After a decade between London and Paris, her work sits at the intersection of restorative craft and digital precision — every smile planned in software, finished by hand.

Founding member, European Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry

Lecturer, Digital Smile Design — annual residency

Author, “On Proportion and Enamel” (2022)

Promise

If a treatment doesn't feel right, we'll say so — and suggest nothing instead.
