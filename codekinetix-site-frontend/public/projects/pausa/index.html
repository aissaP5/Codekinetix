01 — The Ritual Prends ton temps

The city runs on coffee. Pausa runs on the pause.

Pausa is a coffee house built around one thing: the ritual. The espresso that interrupts your morning properly. The Turkish coffee that refuses to be hurried. The corner table where one cappuccino somehow turns into three hours of good conversation.

The beans are fresh, the grind is dialed in every morning, and the baristas actually care whether your flat white is flat enough. Come alone with a book, come with the whole crew — the coffee is serious, nothing else is.
