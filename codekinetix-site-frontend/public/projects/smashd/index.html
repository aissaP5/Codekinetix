THESTACK

Four ingredients that do the heavy lifting. Everything else is just there to hold them together.

01

BUTTER LETTUCE

Hand-torn every hour, never shredded. If it doesn't snap when you bite, it doesn't make the burger. We taste it every shift — the day it stops being crisp is the day we close the kitchen.

Real layer
02

HEIRLOOM TOMATO

Vine-ripened and cut twelve millimeters thick — thick enough to matter, thin enough to melt into the stack. Never refrigerated, because cold is where flavor goes to die.

Real layer
03

AGED CHEDDAR

Eighteen months in the cave, grated fresh, laid double. It doesn't just melt — it cascades. Watch it drape over the patty like it was born there.

Real layer
04

SMASHED ANGUS

A 200g blend of dry-aged chuck and brisket, smashed hard on a 200°C plancha for ten seconds. That thin, lacy, caramelized crust is the whole point — the maillard reaction doesn't negotiate.
