    Our Story

    “Born from a passion for exceptional ingredients and meaningful moments, Bistro brings contemporary cuisine into a warm and intimate setting.”

    What began as a twelve-table room on 12 Rue Montorgueil is still, at heart, exactly that — a dining room where the brioche is baked at dawn, the beef is dry-aged in the cellar, and the kitchen is two steps away.

    Marie & Étienne Moreau — Founders
